The challenge

Evaluate a majority acquisition for a financial-sector acquirer. The team screened potential targets against strategic fit, financial attractiveness, execution risks and regulatory considerations.

The approach

Compared acquisition candidates, constructed valuation frameworks using discounted cash flow, trading comparables and precedent transactions, and assessed financing and accretion/dilution.

What it demonstrates

Financial modelling, strategic judgement, presentation design and collaborative investment analysis. Detailed transaction materials are not published while competition confidentiality and release permissions remain unconfirmed.
