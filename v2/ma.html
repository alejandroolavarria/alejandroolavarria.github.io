The challenge

Evaluate a majority acquisition for a financial-sector acquirer. The team screened potential targets against strategic fit, financial attractiveness, execution risks and regulatory considerations.

The approach

Screened European and international brokers, then valued flatexDEGIRO using FCFE, trading comparables and precedent transaction premiums. The deck estimates a standalone value of €37.15 per share, with 40% cash and 60% debt funding and 2.57% recurring FY2028 EPS accretion. The recommendation was to proceed to diligence, subject to founder support, regulatory approvals and downside underwriting.
